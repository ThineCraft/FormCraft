ThineCraft project scope

FormCraft

Status: Placeholder project. This document describes intended direction; it does not claim that working functionality exists.

Project website

Purpose

FormCraft is the planned open forms platform for moving one traceable record between structured data, a fillable PDF, a browser, printed paper, guided capture, and ShareCraft.

Confirmed direction

  • One form definition usable for browser entry, fillable PDFs, printed paper, and subsequent electronic recovery.
  • PdfCraft rendering and editing for fillable forms, including a browser-based read-only view for completed records.
  • Electronic submissions into ShareCraft through a controlled public surface that does not expose the private workspace.
  • A unique QR code on each printed document instance, used as an alignment anchor and opaque identity for the instance, template version, field map, and ShareCraft record.
  • A PdfCraft phone experience that streams guided video while the user moves over the page, assembles a panorama-like capture, and progressively fills the electronic record.
  • Optional digital signing and OpenTimestamps submission for a Bitcoin-anchored proof that a finalized document hash existed.
  • Document contents and personal information remain off the Bitcoin ledger.

Boundaries and non-goals

  • The QR code identifies and aligns a specific document instance; it must not expose personal information.
  • The phone workflow is not a single-photo scan. It is a guided video/panorama capture intended to reconstruct and interpret the page progressively.
  • Bitcoin is used only as an optional public timestamp anchor for a cryptographic hash, not as document storage or payment processing.
  • PdfCraft supplies document rendering and capture capabilities; ShareCraft supplies governed storage and submission boundaries.
  • No implementation, recognition accuracy, legal-signature status, or production readiness is currently promised.

Expected integrations

  • PdfCraft for fillable PDFs, browser rendering, read-only views, mobile capture, and document signing.
  • ShareCraft for controlled public intake, private records, permissions, retention, and workflow context.
  • FlowCraft for review, approval, routing, and exception handling.
  • OpenTimestamps and the Bitcoin ledger for optional hash attestation.

Open decisions

  • Canonical form schema, field maps, template versions, calculations, validation, and accessibility.
  • QR payload, opaque identity resolution, anti-tamper measures, privacy, and offline behavior.
  • Video transport, panorama reconstruction, page geometry, handwriting/mark recognition, and human correction.
  • Digital-signature model, signer identity, evidence package, timestamp verification, and jurisdictional claims.
  • Submission authentication, malware protection, rate limits, retention, and error recovery.

Guidance for AI contributors

  1. Keep the browser, PDF, paper, QR, guided capture, and governed submission paths unified around one record.
  2. Do not reduce guided paper capture to a single photograph.
  3. Never propose putting document contents or personal information on a public ledger.
  4. Do not claim recognition accuracy, legal effect, or implemented functionality without evidence.
  5. Record new product decisions in this file before presenting them as settled scope.
